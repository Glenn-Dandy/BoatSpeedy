1.3.0
* Smart auto-pause: only pauses when the current is low AND the boat is still, so drifting keeps recording; both thresholds configurable (speed 0 = ignore), plus an on/off switch
* Dashboard status chip: tap to override auto-pause or re-enable it
* Notifications: own settings entry, optional always-on, freely selectable values, two lines
* Low-charge and charge-level alerts now loop until acknowledged (banner, volume key or "Silence")
* Merge selected trips into one; sharing several trips now creates one GPX file per trip
* Battery: short auto-name, type and MAC in the details, rename
* Fixed weather radar loading in stages and auto-pause not re-evaluating after toggling
