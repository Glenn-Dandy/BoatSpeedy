1.3.4
New
* Redodo, LiTime and Power Queen batteries are supported and verified on real hardware
* Charge cycles and total discharge in a new History section of the battery card
* Rated capacity shown with the battery type
* Hidden BLE diagnostic for reporting unsupported batteries (seven taps on the version in About)
Changed
* The rain radar is drawn like the DWD's own app: round outlines with crisp colour edges
* The radar frame shows its clock time, not just how far ahead it is
* Merging and deleting trips now ask for confirmation
Fixed
* The radar appears in a second or two instead of after several loops
* The radar no longer ages while the map stays open, and stays put while panning and zooming
* Remaining charge no longer shows a reference capacity smaller than the charge itself
